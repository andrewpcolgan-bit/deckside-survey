The Problem

01

Coaching tools are built for coaches. Swimmers have almost no visibility into their own data, workload, or development.

02

What happens on the deck stays on the deck. Practice data lives in spreadsheets coaches rarely revisit, and the insights that could shape future training never get captured.

03

The most important conversations in swimming happen in 30-second windows on the pool deck. Without a way to spark or continue those conversations, breakthroughs get lost in the noise.

What I'm Building

The all-in-one platform for coaches and swimmers.

Deckside connects practice planning, attendance, yardage tracking, and athlete communication into one place, so coaches spend less time managing tools and more time coaching. And for the first time, swimmers get visibility into their own data.
